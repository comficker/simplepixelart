# Colour pages (/arts/color-<hex>, every locale) are a crawl trap: each art
# links ~16 hex swatches, so the space is near-endless. On 2026-10-18 they
# took >50% of all crawling (Claude-SearchBot ~40k/day, Googlebot ~20k/day)
# for 7 search clicks in 90 days, and kept the SSR process restarting.
# Disallowed for every crawler (no separate Googlebot/Bingbot groups: a named
# group makes that bot ignore these rules), so crawl goes to real art pages.
Disallow: /arts/color-
Disallow: /*/arts/color-
Disallow: /*?sort=
Disallow: /*&sort=
